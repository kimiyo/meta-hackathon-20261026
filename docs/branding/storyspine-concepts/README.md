Brand metaphor: spine of a book and backbone of a coherent story. Palette warm ivory #F7F4EC, forest green #214E40, dark ink #202C29, restrained brass #A77B3E. Premium literary editorial design, generous whitespace, highly intentional typography and composition. Hero and presentation text only "StorySpine" and "Give your story a spine." Keep labels sparse, English only, no tiny paragraphs. Brand mark must be bold, distinctive, simple enough for 16px, no fine-line details. Keep the core logo strictly flat, with no shadows or gradients; dimensional illustration may appear only in hero art. No Meta logos, no Muse wording, no robot, no brain, no anatomical spine, no stock quill or lightbulb. Do not invent product UI features. Maintain same logo shape across every application. Direction A, titled "01 / BOOKSPINE". Build a strong geometric abstract book-spine symbol: a solid vertical spine joined to three thick, offset page/chapter bars, subtly suggesting an S through negative space. A compact bookbinding printer's mark, confident and quiet. Wordmark in refined contemporary editorial serif. Hero artwork: sculptural ivory folded pages arranged around a central forest-green binding, visually suggesting multiple chapters held together; subtle paper grain, soft editorial light. Beautiful design studio presentation board, no busy decorations.
